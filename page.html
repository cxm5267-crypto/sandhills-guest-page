The Week Ahead in the Sandhills

Updated Sunday, October 18

Pinehurst, Southern Pines, Aberdeen and the rest of Moore County, day by day. The last stretch before Halloween, and the calendar shows it: pumpkin patches, three outdoor film screenings, Oktoberfest in the Village and a corn maze you reach by trolley. The week ahead is dry and sunny with highs near 70°, so most of this works outdoors — though nights fall to the mid 40s, and a jacket after sunset is the one thing worth planning for.

All week, whenever you like

The Sandhills Scarecrow Stroll is in its last days at the Sandhills Horticultural Gardens, 3245 Airport Road, Pinehurst — scarecrows built as Revolutionary-era characters by local clubs and businesses, free, 8am to 7pm daily, closing November 1. You can vote for a favourite, and there is a Kid's Choice category. Nearby, the Sandhills Woman's Exchange at 15 Azalea Road has its Pumpkin Explosion in the patch around the old log cabin, to October 31, and Dewey's Veggie Vibe in West End opens its pumpkin patch daily 8am–8pm with the rows lit from 6pm for night picking — leashed dogs are welcome on the farm grounds.

Two free gallery shows are hanging. "Driven Beauty", car culture in photographs, paintings and mixed media, is at the Arts Council Galleries in Campbell House, 482 E. Connecticut Avenue, Southern Pines, weekdays 10am–5pm until October 30. "Inspired by Land & Sea" is at the Exchange Street Gallery of Fine Art, 129 Exchange Street, Aberdeen, Monday to Saturday 12–3pm, and closes on Friday October 23. Both are reasons to walk the two downtowns slowly — Broad Street in Southern Pines puts the galleries, the Country Bookshop, the antique shops and the Sunrise Theater within a few blocks of each other, while the Village of Pinehurst is at its best early in the morning or late in the afternoon, when it is less crowded.

Carolina Cinemas runs Flashback Cinema nightly at 7pm from Friday with "Scream" at thirty, and the Sunrise has "Sense and Sensibility" on Sunday, Wednesday, Thursday and again on the 28th. The Shaw House grounds are open for free tours Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 1–4pm. If you are here with a dog and out for a long day, Waggin' Wagon is running pumpkin-patch field trips on Wednesday and Friday, collecting your dog from where you are staying and bringing them back tired; Martin Park on Commerce Avenue in Southern Pines has 50 off-lead acres of wooded trails and fields, dawn to dusk and free, though it is only partly fenced, so your dog needs good recall — park in the cul-de-sac rather than the Yadkin Plaza lot, where you risk being towed.

Eat & drink

  • Southern Pines Wine Cellar, Southern Pines — an intimate wine bar with small bites, and live music on Friday and Saturday through the whole run of this page.
  • Chapman's Food and Spirits, Pinehurst — relaxed, plenty of outdoor seating, loaded nachos. Right for a 70° afternoon.
  • Pinehurst Brewing Company, in the Village — get a flight. Two minutes from Saturday's Oktoberfest, and there is live music in the beer garden on Halloween.
  • The Ice Cream Parlor, Broad Street, Southern Pines — a real soda fountain, on that street since 1976. Honey lavender in a cone. Expect a line, especially early in the evening.
  • Mulligans Creamery — small-batch, and it leans all the way into the golf theme: scoops are ordered as a bogey, a double bogey or a birdie. Vegan and dairy-free options.
  • Midland Bistro, Southern Pines — consistently good; the tuna melt.
  • Betsy's Crepes, Southern Pines — quick service, sweet and savoury. Nutella and banana.
  • Rec Room Bar and Arcade, Southern Pines — Pac-Man and Galaga alongside the craft beer, and the home of Saturday's Knight Market.

Getting around

Columbus Drive in Aberdeen is closed near Aberdeen Pines Way, between U.S. 15-501 and South Hill Road, until November 30, with the detour signed along U.S. 15-501, Johnson Street and South Hill Road. Single lanes on U.S. 15-501 between Turner Street and U.S. 1 also close nightly from 8pm to 6am through the same date, so an evening drive between Aberdeen and Southern Pines can be slower than it looks. Midland Road is being milled and resurfaced between Airport Road and Clark Street, with periodic lane closures in the off-peak daytime hours — allow a few extra minutes between Pinehurst and Southern Pines in the middle of the day.

Two evenings draw a crowd into a downtown. Downtown Southern Pines fills from 5pm on Friday October 23, with trick-or-treating along the shops and the film at the Downtown Park afterwards. The Village of Pinehurst fills from 4pm on Saturday October 24 for Oktoberfest at Tufts Memorial Park, and again on the evening of Friday October 30 for the film on the Village Green — parking downtown is free, and earlier is easier either way.
