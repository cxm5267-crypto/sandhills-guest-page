The Week Ahead in the Sandhills

Updated Sunday, October 4

What is on in Pinehurst, Southern Pines, Aberdeen and the rest of Moore County over the coming week — markets and music, galleries and festivals, with times, places and prices. The nights are turning properly cool now, down near 46° midweek, while afternoons still sit in the mid-to-high 70s, so bring a jacket for anything outdoors after sunset.

All week, whenever you like

The Sandhills Scarecrow Stroll is up at the Sandhills Horticultural Gardens, 3245 Airport Rd, Pinehurst — twenty scarecrows dressed as characters from the Revolutionary era, free, 8am to 7pm daily through November 1.

Three gallery shows are hanging, all free. “Driven Beauty” at the Arts Council Galleries in Campbell House, 482 E Connecticut Ave, Southern Pines, turns car culture into art — photographs by David J. Knight, paintings by Lory Lockwood and mixed-media pieces by Tony Watts, weekdays 10am–5pm through October 30, plus Saturday October 17 from 10am to 2pm. “Inspired by Land & Sea” at the Exchange Street Gallery in historic downtown Aberdeen runs Monday to Saturday, 12–3pm, through October 23. And “Frank Pierce: Crossroads” at the Jeanne Hastings Gallery in Pinehurst closes October 12.

Carolina Cinemas on Brucewood Drive is running Flashback Cinema nightly at 7:30pm all month — “The Shining” through Thursday October 8, then “Beetlejuice” from Friday to Thursday October 15. Those and the galleries are the indoor fallbacks when an evening turns wet.

Outdoors, Weymouth Woods opens at 8am daily, free, with six easy trails through longleaf pines some of which are estimated at nearly 500 years old; the Lighter Stump to Pine Island to Holly Road loop is 3.8 miles. Reservoir Park has a two-mile loop around a 95-acre lake, with fishing, kayaking and an 18-hole disc golf course. Rassie Wicker Park in Pinehurst has paved greenway trails, easy and well-kept, and is a good walk before coffee. There is more in our guide to what to do in Pinehurst if you don't golf.

Eat & drink

Los Jefes has opened at 100 Pavilion Way in Southern Pines, in the space off Morganton Road that Vaquera used to occupy. It comes from the family behind El Patron Tacos & Tequila in Aberdeen and aims a little more upscale: tacos, enchiladas and burritos alongside chilaquiles and tostones rellenos, with cocktails made to order. Open 11am–10pm Monday to Friday, to 11pm Saturday and to 9pm Sunday.

  • Elliot's on Linden, Pinehurst — upscale without being stuffy, with a strong wine list.
  • Warren's Bistro, Southern Pines — small and comfortable, and the French onion soup is the one to order on a cool night.
  • The Sly Fox, 795 SW Broad St, Southern Pines — a British-style gastropub with 16 taps, shepherd's pie and bangers and mash. The move when the weather turns.
  • The Deuce, at the Pinehurst No. 2 clubhouse, 1 Carolina Vista Dr — a terrace over the 18th green. You do not need a tee time on No. 2 to drink here; just turn up, and get the wings.
  • Railhouse Brewery, downtown Aberdeen — veteran-owned, brewing since 2010, 16 taps and full liquor. The cheese curds and the soft pretzel are the standouts, and it is five minutes from Pinehurst.
  • Agora Bakery + Cafe, Pinehurst — family-run inside a historic bank building, more bakery than coffee shop. Go here if you want coffee with a real meal; the spinach pie is excellent.
  • Sweet Carolina, historic downtown Aberdeen — 1950s diner style, and one stop on the Sandhills ice cream tour. Pair it with a walk down Main Street.

Getting around

Midland Road, between Pinehurst and Southern Pines, is being milled and resurfaced, with periodic lane closures during off-peak daytime hours and the work scheduled to run into the autumn. Allow a few extra minutes if you are crossing between the two towns in the middle of the day.

Saturday October 10 is the busiest day downtown: the farmers market holds the Downtown Park in Southern Pines until noon, and Oktoberfest fills Red's Corner on SW Broad Street from 11am to 8pm. The crowd comes back to Red's for Pawtoberfest on Sunday afternoon. Both are easiest before 10am, or late. On Saturday October 17 the Holly Arts & Crafts Festival takes over the Village of Pinehurst from 10am to 4pm, which is the one morning to leave the car outside the circle and walk in.
