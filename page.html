All week, whenever you like

The Sandhills Scarecrow Stroll opens on October 2 at the Sandhills Horticultural Gardens in Pinehurst and runs to November 1 — twenty scarecrows through the gardens, free, 8am to 5pm daily. Two shows are hanging: “Frank Pierce: Crossroads” at the Jeanne Hastings Gallery inside the Boyd Library in Pinehurst, through October 12, and the Halloween Boo-tique free costume shop at the Southern Pines library, 10am–5pm through October 2. Those and the Sunrise Theater are the indoor fallbacks if a day turns wet.

Outdoors, Weymouth Woods in Southern Pines opens at 8am daily and is free — six easy trails through longleaf pines, some of them estimated at nearly 500 years old, and a boardwalk over swampland. The loop to walk is Lighter Stump / Pine Island / Holly Road, 3.8 miles. Dogs on a leash, six feet maximum. Reservoir Park is a flatter two-mile lap of a 95-acre lake, with fishing, kayaking and an 18-hole disc golf course if you want to make a morning of it, and Rassie Wicker Park in Pinehurst has paved greenway trails that are easy walking before coffee.

With a dog, see our guide to visiting with a pet. Martin Park, 350 Commerce Ave in Southern Pines, is 50 off-leash acres of wooded trails, open fields and a creek, dawn to dusk, free — but it is only partly fenced, so your dog needs solid recall, and you should park in the cul-de-sac rather than the Yadkin Plaza lot, where you risk being towed.

The two downtowns are worth an unhurried hour each. Pinehurst Village is genuinely walkable and locally owned, and quietest early morning or late afternoon. Broad Street in Southern Pines is more of a working downtown: The Country Bookshop, the Campbell House galleries, antique shops and the restored 1920s Sunrise Theater.

Eat & drink

Four picks for a warm, dry week, from our own writing on the area:

  • Red's Corner, 901 SW Broad St, Southern Pines — a two-acre beer garden and food truck park with around six trucks rotating, live music Thursday to Sunday in the warmer months, and a play area for children. Download their app for the day's lineup and to pre-order, and bring a blanket.
  • Drum & Quill, Pinehurst Village — pub food, ten outdoor tables, good fish and chips, open late at weekends. The fried okra with Green Goddess dressing is worth the trip on its own.
  • Railhouse Brewery, downtown Aberdeen — brewing since 2010, veteran-owned, 16 taps plus full liquor, family and dog friendly, five minutes from Pinehurst. Get the cheese curds and the soft pretzel. More on the local beer scene here.
  • The Ice Cream Parlor, Broad St, Southern Pines — a real soda fountain, on that street since 1976. Honey lavender in a cone. Expect a line, especially early evening. Seven more stops on the Sandhills ice cream tour.

New this autumn: Rec Room on SW Broad Street in Southern Pines is building a kitchen into its back corner for hand-tossed pizza and wings, whole or by the slice, with late hours the point. It may open as early as November. Until then it is arcade games, a full bar and a patio.

Getting around

Two crowds to plan around. On Wednesday September 30, between 5:30 and 7pm, the Pinecrest homecoming parade brings people onto the streets of downtown Southern Pines — park a few blocks off Broad Street if you are eating there. On Saturday October 3 the farmers market and AutumnFest share the Downtown Park from 8am to 4pm, so arrive early or expect to walk a while from wherever you leave the car.
