The Week Ahead in the Sandhills

Updated Sunday, October 25

What is on across Pinehurst, Southern Pines, Aberdeen and the rest of Moore County over the coming week — markets, music, galleries, a train you eat dinner on, and a Halloween that falls on a Saturday this year.

The run of weather is dry and warming: shirtsleeves by late morning, and cold enough after dark early in the week to want a real jacket before it softens towards the weekend. The live forecast below the listings is the one to trust.

All week, whenever you like

“Driven Beauty” — photographs, paintings and mixed media all built around cars — hangs free at the Arts Council galleries in Campbell House, Southern Pines, weekdays 10am–5pm, and comes down on Friday October 30. It is reason enough to walk Broad Street slowly: the galleries, The Country Bookshop and the Sunrise Theater sit within a few blocks of each other, which also makes them the obvious fallbacks if a day turns wet.

The pumpkin places run right up to Halloween. Dewey’s Veggie Vibe in West End has its patch and farm shop open daily 8am–8pm, with the lights on from 6pm and leashed dogs welcome on the grounds; the Sandhills Woman’s Exchange has pumpkins around its log cabin at 15 Azalea Road, 10am–3pm Monday to Saturday and 10am–2pm Sunday, through October 31. The Sandhills Scarecrow Stroll at the Horticultural Gardens in Pinehurst is free, 8am–7pm, and finishes on November 1.

For the rest of Halloween week: the Aberdeen Fear Factory opens nightly at 8pm, with the ticket window from 7:30pm and $10 parking, and the drive-by haunted cemetery on Oakmont Circle in Pinehurst runs 7–9pm, free, and is meant to be seen from the street. The Shaw House grounds are free to tour Thursday to Saturday, 1–4pm.

If you are here with a dog, Saturday’s doggy trick-or-treat at Moore Equine is the easy one, and there is more on the area with a pet: Martin Park in Southern Pines gives 50 wooded acres off-leash — park in the cul-de-sac, not the Yadkin Plaza lot, where you risk being towed — and Pooch Park in the Pines in Whispering Pines is fully fenced with separate small and large sides. It runs on memberships, but it is worth asking about a day pass if you are only visiting.

Eat & drink

Our longer guides: coffee in Pinehurst and Southern Pines, where to drink local beer and the Sandhills ice cream tour.

  • Swank Coffee Shoppe, Southern Pines — relaxed, with outdoor seating this week’s weather is made for. Ask for the Green Goblin Scone.
  • Red’s Corner, SW Broad Street, Southern Pines — a two-acre beer garden and food truck park, around six trucks rotating, with a kids’ play area alongside. Download their app for the day’s line-up and to pre-order. Bring a blanket.
  • Midland Bistro, Southern Pines — consistently good. The tuna melt.
  • Scrollicious, Pinehurst — rolled ice cream, bubble tea and snow ice, and a change from the usual options if you are in the Village.
  • The Deuce, Pinehurst No. 2 clubhouse, 1 Carolina Vista Drive — a terrace over the 18th green. You do not need a tee time on No. 2 to drink here; just turn up. Get the wings.
  • Chapman’s Food and Spirits, Pinehurst — relaxed, outdoor seating, loaded nachos.
  • Mulligans Creamery — small-batch ice cream that leans all the way into the golf theme: scoops come as a bogey, a double bogey or a birdie. Vegan and dairy-free options.
  • Betsy’s Crepes, Southern Pines — quick, sweet or savoury. Nutella and banana.
  • Bell Tree Tavern, NE Broad Street, Southern Pines — three reasons to go this week, and dogs are welcome at the outdoor tables so long as they stay on the ground.

Out on the courses

Nothing is closed and nothing is being aerated or overseeded in the week ahead. The one thing worth knowing is that The Cradle — the 18-hole par-three course on the resort grounds, and the easiest way onto the property without a full tee time — gives itself over to the monthly Knockout from 4:30pm on Wednesday November 4. If you were planning a late loop that afternoon, go earlier. There is also plenty of golf here without a resort package, including three top-100 public courses.

Getting around

Columbus Drive in Aberdeen stays closed near Aberdeen Pines Way, between U.S. 15-501 and South Hill Road, until November 30; the signed detour runs U.S. 15-501, Johnson Street and South Hill Road. On the same project, single lanes on U.S. 15-501 between Turner Street and U.S. 1 close nightly from 8pm to 6am, also through November 30. Midland Road between Airport Road and Clark Street is still being milled and resurfaced, with periodic lane closures in off-peak daytime hours and no detour in place, so allow a few extra minutes between Pinehurst and Southern Pines in the middle of the day.

For crowds rather than cones: the Village of Pinehurst fills from about 6pm on Friday for the film on the Village Green, where parking is free throughout downtown and golf carts go in the pine straw behind the Given Memorial Library. Both downtowns are busy on Halloween night. And on Saturday November 7, downtown Southern Pines closes around the Veterans Parade from mid-morning — if you want Broad Street that day, be parked before 9:30am.
