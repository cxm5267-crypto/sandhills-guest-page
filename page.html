All week, whenever you like

The Sandhills Scarecrow Stroll runs from Friday at the Sandhills Horticultural Gardens in Pinehurst — twenty scarecrows on a Revolutionary-era theme, free, 8am–5pm daily through November 1, with a costume stroll on October 24. “Frank Pierce: Crossroads” hangs at the Jeanne Hastings Gallery in the Boyd Library at Sandhills Community College, free during library hours, to October 12. Carolina Cinemas has “The Return of the King” at 2:30pm daily in its final week, then “Beetlejuice” from October 9. Those three are the fallbacks if a wet afternoon arrives.

Outdoors, Weymouth Woods opens at 8am and is free — six easy trails through longleaf pines, some of them close to 500 years old, and the loop to walk is Lighter Stump to Pine Island to Holly Road, 3.8 miles. Reservoir Park has a 2-mile loop around the lake, fishing, kayaking and a disc golf course. With a dog, Martin Park on Commerce Avenue in Southern Pines is 50 off-leash acres of woods and creek — park in the cul-de-sac, not the Yadkin Plaza lot, which tows, and note the park is only partly fenced.

Eat & drink

  • The Sly Fox, 795 SW Broad St, Southern Pines — British-style gastropub, 16 taps, shepherd’s pie, poutine and bangers and mash. The move when the weather turns.
  • BHAWK, 175 Yadkin Rd, Southern Pines — distillery, cocktail bar and restaurant with a shaded back patio. The burger and the BLT both land; reservations are worth making for dinner. The Gratitude Room, a small museum honouring military service, is worth walking through even if you are not drinking.
  • Agora Bakery + Cafe, Pinehurst — family-run, inside a historic bank building, more bakery than coffee shop. Go here when you want coffee with a real meal; the spinach pie is excellent and the macarons have a following.
  • Sweet Carolina, historic downtown Aberdeen — 1950s diner-style ice cream, five minutes from Saturday’s strongman competition. Pair it with a walk down Main Street.
  • Railhouse Brewery, downtown Aberdeen — veteran-owned, brewing since 2010, 16 taps plus full liquor. Cheese curds and the soft pretzel are the standouts, and it is family and dog friendly, five minutes from Pinehurst.

Rec Room on SW Broad Street in Southern Pines is building a pizza-and-wings kitchen into its back corner, but not before November — for now it stays arcade games, a full bar and a late licence.

Getting around

No road closures affect the area this week, but two days bring crowds. On Wednesday September 30, 5:30–7pm, the Pinecrest homecoming parade fills downtown Southern Pines — park off Broad Street if you are eating there. On Saturday October 3 the farmers market and AutumnFest share the Southern Pines Downtown Park from 8am to 4pm, while Odin’s Arena takes S. Sycamore Street in downtown Aberdeen for the same hours. Both are easiest before 10am.
